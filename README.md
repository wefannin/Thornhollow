# Thornhollow


A small open-world fantasy RPG that runs in the browser, built with Three.js. Explore a valley, help the villagers of Thornhollow, reforge the broken sword Dakorosqa, and face the dragon of Skarnveld Spire.

Playing

Open thornhollow.html in a modern desktop browser and click Enter the valley. Click the game view to capture the mouse. If your browser blocks mouse capture (for example, inside some embedded frames), the game switches to drag-to-look instead.

Controls
Movement
Key	Action
W A S D	Move
Shift	Sprint (uses stamina)
Space	Jump
Mouse	Look around
Combat
Key	Action
Left click	Strike with your weapon
Right click	Cast the selected spell
1 – 4	Select a spell
R	Cycle to the next spell
Q	Drink a health potion
Interaction and menus
Key	Action
E	Talk, search a body, open a chest, use an object
Tab or I	Open your pack (items, skills, journal, map)
J	Open the journal
M	Open the map (fast travel to discovered places)
Esc	Pause or close a menu
F3 or `	Toggle the performance overlay
Touch devices

Use the left stick to move, drag on the right side of the screen to look, and tap the on-screen buttons to strike, cast, jump, switch spells, interact, and open your pack.

Tips
Skills improve as you use them. Swinging trains One-handed, casting trains Destruction or Restoration, and trading trains Speech.
Some enemies resist certain magic. Watch for "Immune" when you hit them.
Only silver, or a blade of legend, can finish off the Lycan.
Progress saves automatically.
